FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the Address Gateway team with no experience?
Apply through the track that matches your interest — no prior experience is required. We review applications weekly and select for motivation and potential, not a finished CV.
Is the Address Gateway Growth Accelerator paid?
Yes — from day one, there’s a real earning opportunity tied directly to your performance, alongside structured training, mentoring, and certification. Strong performers move straight into employment and funded projects after the 3 months.
How long is the training programme?
Three months, with applications reviewed on a rolling weekly basis — there’s no fixed intake date to wait for.
What happens after the 3 months?
Top performers are considered for employment, paid project work, or funding support, based on performance and available opportunities at the time.
Can I apply if I already have a job or business?
Yes. The Founder and Co-founder tracks, and the Affiliate and Ambassador programmes, are built for people who don’t want a traditional role.
What consulting services does Address Gateway offer in Qatar?
Business consulting, staff training, and in-person workshops — delivered directly by the team running Address Gateway’s own growth strategy.
How do I become an Address Gateway affiliate or ambassador?
Apply through the Promote & Earn section. Affiliates earn commission on referred business; ambassadors get visibility and perks for representing the brand.
I have a startup idea — can Address Gateway help me build it?
Yes — pitch it through the Founder track for mentorship and a path toward incubation support.
